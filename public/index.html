Gestures

Touch mode (finger = mouse pointer, 1:1)

  • Tap — left click
  • Drag — move / drag & drop
  • Hold ½ second — right click
  • Two fingers drag — scroll the desktop
  • Two fingers tap — right click
  • Pinch — zoom the view

Trackpad mode (relative, precise)

  • Move finger — move the pointer
  • Tap — left click
  • Two fingers drag — scroll
  • Two fingers tap — right click

When zoomed in, drag with two fingers to pan the view.
